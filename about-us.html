About Us

Established as an Authorised Channel Partner for M/s. Johnson & Johnson - Medical in 1996, we have expanded our services to major institutions, leading nursing homes, and dialysis centers. Our dedicated and experienced sales and service team is committed to delivering the best in the industry.

In 2001, we began handling CAPD products from M/s. Baxter (India) Pvt. Ltd., serving around 150 dialysis patients and major renal centers in Chennai. We manage operations from our 4,000 Sq.Ft. warehouse with our own transport and crew for prompt delivery.

We launched marketing and sales for Fresenius Medical Care - Dialysis Machines in 2013, and expanded our operations to South Tamil Nadu with a new Madurai warehouse in 2016, supported by a dedicated sales team and administrative staff.

Our CSR efforts include co-organizing Nursing and Operation Theater programs focusing on skill development and advanced sterilization techniques through the Nursing Home Division of Johnson & Johnson.
